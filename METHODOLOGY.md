# House of Cican — Research Methodology

## Mission

House of Cican is a living, global, evidence-first archive dedicated to the history of the Cican surname and the people, families, places, documents, migrations and historical contexts associated with it.

Core principle: **LEAVE NO CICAN BEHIND.**

The archive aims to document the surname from its earliest defensible evidence through 2026 and future generations.

## Evidence hierarchy

- **A — Original evidence:** original archival record, manuscript, register, contemporary document, original photograph, or equivalent primary evidence.
- **B — Reliable archival/index evidence:** institutional catalogue, archive index, or authoritative finding aid.
- **C — Credible secondary source:** scholarly book, academic article, reputable historical publication.
- **D — Genealogical contribution:** family tree or researcher contribution not yet independently proven.
- **E — Unverified information:** online claim lacking sufficient evidence.
- **F — Research lead:** potentially useful clue requiring investigation.

Evidence level is assigned to individual claims where possible, not as a blanket score for an entire person.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Never invent a relationship.
2. Never merge people automatically.
3. Preserve historical spellings exactly as found.
4. Separate original evidence from indexes, transcriptions and later interpretations.
5. Trace repeated online claims toward their underlying source.
6. Record contradictions rather than silently choosing a preferred version.
7. Distinguish earliest discovered evidence from absolute origin.
8. Respect copyright, archive terms and privacy.
9. Minimize public information about living people.
10. Preserve an audit trail for significant changes.
11. Never sacrifice accuracy for the number of records collected.

## Historical scope

The research may examine deep human, linguistic, geographic and historical context when relevant to understanding the surname. Prehistoric or ancient material must never be presented as evidence for the Cican surname unless a defensible evidentiary connection exists.

## Ancestry and restricted sources

Ancestry and other subscription services are sources, not authorities. Records supplied by the project owner from authorized access may be analyzed, transcribed, compared with independent sources and cited according to their evidentiary value. No scraping, bypassing or unauthorized access is permitted.

## Privacy

Exact residential addresses and other sensitive information about living people are private research data and must not be published in the public repository unless there is a clear lawful and appropriate reason and explicit authorization. Public records may still be cited without unnecessarily reproducing sensitive details.
