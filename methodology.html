RESEARCH
METHODOLOGY

House of Cican is a living global archive dedicated to documenting people, documents, places, migrations and historical contexts associated with the Cican surname. The archive is designed to preserve uncertainty rather than turn clues into facts.

Evidence hierarchy

A — Original evidenceOriginal archival records, manuscripts, registers, contemporary documents and equivalent primary evidence.
B — Archival / index evidenceInstitutional catalogues, archive indexes and authoritative finding aids.
C — Credible secondary sourceScholarly books, academic articles and reputable historical publications.
D — Genealogical contributionFamily trees or researcher contributions not yet independently proven.
E — Unverified informationPublic claims lacking sufficient evidence.
F — Research leadA potentially useful clue requiring further investigation.

Non-negotiable rules

  1. Never invent a relationship.
  2. Never merge people automatically.
  3. Preserve historical spellings exactly as found.
  4. Separate original evidence from indexes, transcriptions and later interpretations.
  5. Trace repeated online claims toward their underlying source.
  6. Record contradictions instead of silently choosing a preferred version.
  7. Distinguish earliest discovered evidence from absolute origin.
  8. Respect copyright, archive terms and privacy.
  9. Minimize public information about living people.
  10. Preserve an audit trail for significant changes.
  11. Never sacrifice accuracy for the number of records collected.

Historical scope

The archive may examine linguistic, geographic and historical context when relevant. Ancient or prehistoric material is not treated as evidence for the Cican surname unless a defensible evidentiary connection exists.

Privacy and restricted sources

Subscription genealogy services are sources, not authorities. Authorized records may be analyzed and compared with independent evidence. No scraping, bypassing or unauthorized access is permitted. Exact residential addresses and sensitive information about living people are not published by default.

Research status

Records may be classified as Documented, Probable, Possible, Unverified or Research Lead. A research lead can remain open indefinitely when the available evidence does not justify a stronger conclusion.
