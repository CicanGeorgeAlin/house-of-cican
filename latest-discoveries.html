05 OCTOBER 2026

IURGHICENI 1657–1669 PROPERTY DOSSIER EXPANDED

BNaR’s digitized-document inventory reveals a substantial intervening Iurghiceni property cluster between the 1547 charter and the 1673 register. The newly verified targets include 1657 documents concerning Ionașco Bandurul and Nicolae Buhuş, 1660–1661 sales of Iurghiceni ancestral/property shares, and a 1669 record naming Constantin son of Vasile of Iurghiceni and grandson of Vasco cel bătrân. These records may help reconstruct property ownership and family branches, but no Cican/Illea continuity is claimed from them.

STATUS: INTERVENING PROPERTY-CHAIN TARGETS · CANDIDATE BRIDGE UNPROVEN
05 OCTOBER 2026

I/227–I/228 DIGITIZATION CONFIRMED

BNaR’s official digitized-document inventories independently list I/227 and I/228 as digitized holdings: I/227 is an undated list of Iurghiceni estate documents, while I/228 is the 22 April 1673 list of 19 purchase deeds for Iurghiceni and Doroșcani. This confirms that the targeted archival items are in the digitized-document programme, but the public inventory does not expose their manuscript text. No Cican/Illea occurrence is therefore claimed from these items yet.

STATUS: ARCHIVAL RETRIEVAL TARGET CONFIRMED · CONTENT STILL TO BE EXTRACTED
04 OCTOBER 2026

1547 ATTESTATION PROVENANCE STRENGTHENED

The current Biblioteca Națională a României inventory independently confirms the 1547 Cican/Iurghiceni archival item and identifies it as an original Slavonic document. This strengthens archival provenance for the earliest-attestation benchmark without creating a second occurrence.

STATUS: OFFICIAL ARCHIVAL PROVENANCE CONFIRMED
04 OCTOBER 2026

1888–1889 CORCEA SCHOOL CICAN RECORDS FOUND

A near-contemporary educational publication lists Nicola Cican, Dina Cecan and Pilea Cican among pupils at the Romanian school in Ghiorgea (Corcea) during the 1888–1889 school year. This may provide the earliest documentary bridge yet found to the later Corcea Cican cluster, but the identities have deliberately not been merged.

STATUS: CRITICAL IDENTITY-BRIDGE INVESTIGATION OPEN
04 OCTOBER 2026

NICOLAE–SPIRU SIBLING BRIDGE FOUND

A 1915 published testimony explicitly identifies Spiru Cican as Nicolae Cican’s brother and tenant in Nicolae’s Corcea house. This is the first direct sibling evidence currently in the archive connecting two of the existing 1903 Cican records. Dina Cican appears in the same source, but her exact relationship remains unproven.

STATUS: DIRECT SIBLING EVIDENCE · DINA RELATIONSHIP UNRESOLVED
04 OCTOBER 2026

NICOLAE CICAN WWI DEATH RECORD CORROBORATED

A contemporary 20 February 1918 issue of România Nouă reports that sub-lieutenant Nicolae Cican, a Romanian from Macedonia, died suddenly in Chișinău on 19 February 1918 and announces his funeral. This provides the archive’s strongest current evidence for his exact death date, place and military rank.

STATUS: CONTEMPORARY DEATH NOTICE · MILITARY RECORD RECOVERY STILL OPEN
04 OCTOBER 2026

CICANI VARIANT IDENTITY LEAD OPENED

A 1936 bibliography records a bust of nationalist fighter Nicolae Cicani from Frașari. The spelling is preserved exactly and has not been merged with the archive’s Nicolae Cican record. The original 1936 article is now a high-priority recovery target.

STATUS: VARIANT OCCURRENCE · IDENTITY UNRESOLVED
04 OCTOBER 2026

NICOLAE CICAN 1914 ACCOUNT CORROBORATED

A contemporary 1914 issue of Românul independently places Nicolae Cican in Corcea during the March 1914 violence and reports that his house was burned while he and his family survived. This strengthens the later scholarly account of his return to Romania and the events preceding his later military service.

STATUS: CONTEMPORARY NEWSPAPER + SCHOLARLY EVIDENCE
04 OCTOBER 2026

NICOLAE CICAN LIFE-COURSE EVIDENCE EXPANDED

Scholarly historical material now documents Nicolae Cican beyond the 1903 founding event: his long residence in America, return to Romania around 1913–1914, and later service in the Romanian Army during the First World War. The archive has opened a primary-record recovery lead for his military service and death.

STATUS: SECONDARY EVIDENCE · PRIMARY MILITARY RECORD SEARCH OPEN

1547 Iurghiceni continuity audit

The 26 May 1547 charter is unusually precise about the transaction: Toader son of Maria sold his right in one-fifth of one-quarter of Iurghiceni on the Bahlui to Cicican and his son Illea for 60 Tatar zlotys. The published edition does not provide a boundary list in the surviving text used here; it says the share is “the part of his share that will be selected,” so the present evidence does not yet provide a named-neighbour chain for following the land forward.

BNaR's inventory confirms the original is item I/5, an original Slavonic document on paper with princely seal. The next located Iurghiceni-related entries do not name Cican/Cicican or Illea, so they are retained as negative controls rather than treated as continuity.

Status: earliest documentary Cican/Cicican attestation remains 1547; property continuity to later Iurghiceni owners remains unproven.
