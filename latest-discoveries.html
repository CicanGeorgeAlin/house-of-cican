1575 IURGHICENI ESTATE OPENS A NEW BRIDGE TEST

BNaR I/9–I/10, both dated 2 August 1575, identify Drăghici’s estate in Iurghiceni on the Bahlui. One act states that Drăghici’s grandfather Toader had acquired the land from Ștefan Vv. cel Bătrân; the companion act divides the estate between Condrea and Burlan/Nikita. This is the earliest concrete post-1547 Iurghiceni title layer yet recovered in this branch. It does not identify the 1547 Cican/Cicican share, and Toader is not to be merged with Toader son of Maria without documentary proof.

1640 COURT CASE VALIDATES THE BALTAG–BENEA TITLE CHAIN

A 27 July 1640 judgment states that Băltag acquired the Iurghiceni parcel from Benea and other relatives. Bandur’s widow challenged the 1638 transfer as a pledge, but Buhuș produced the Bandur deed and additional official documents, and the court rejected her claim. This independently validates the existence of the Benea → Băltag → Bandur → Buhuș title chain. It remains a later parcel chain and is not connected to Cican/Cicican–Illea.

STATUS: JUDICIAL TITLE VALIDATION · BENEA/1547 CONNECTION UNPROVEN

1632 IURGHICENI FRACTIONAL PROPERTY CHAIN RECOVERED

The full 26 May 1637 act explicitly names Obrejie of Iurghiceni, son of Miron and, separately, Obrejie, son of Lupu. This is a decisive identity-control result: the two men are contemporaries in the same locality and must not be merged. 1645 OBREJIE IDENTITY CORRECTION

The 1645 records sharpen rather than merge the Miron branch. On 22 April 1645, Miron of Iurghiceni is independently active in a dispute over damage caused by his horses. On 21 August 1645, Obrejie and Miron of Iurghiceni appear together as witnesses. However, the 28 November 1644 act explicitly calls Miron's son Obrejie răposatul (deceased). Therefore the living Obrejie named in 1645 cannot be identified automatically with that deceased son. The archive now treats the 1645 Obrejie as a separate identity pending documentary proof.

1657–1669 INHERITANCE FILTER RUN

The Iurghiceni 1657–1669 dossier was re-read specifically for explicit predecessor and inheritance language. The catalogue preserves multiple family structures: children of named parents in 1660–1662 transfers, Agahia as daughter of Lupul of Iurghiceni in 1661, and Constantin son of Vasile, grandson of Vasco cel Bătrân in 1669. These records strengthen the archive's ability to reconstruct inherited shares, but no current 1657–1669 catalogue entry explicitly identifies Cican/Cicican or Illea or proves that any listed share derives from their 1547 1/20. The absence is retained as a search result, not as proof of non-existence.

STATUS: INHERITANCE NETWORK EXPANDED · NO 1547 CICAN LINK FOUND IN THIS FILTER

1547 Iurghiceni continuity audit

The 26 May 1547 charter is unusually precise about the transaction: Toader son of Maria sold his right in one-fifth of one-quarter of Iurghiceni on the Bahlui to Cicican and his son Illea for 60 Tatar zlotys. The published edition does not provide a boundary list in the surviving text used here; it says the share is “the part of his share that will be selected,” so the present evidence does not yet provide a named-neighbour chain for following the land forward.

BNaR's inventory confirms the original is item I/5, an original Slavonic document on paper with princely seal. The next located Iurghiceni-related entries do not name Cican/Cicican or Illea, so they are retained as negative controls rather than treated as continuity.

Status: earliest documentary Cican/Cicican attestation remains 1547; property continuity to later Iurghiceni owners remains unproven.
