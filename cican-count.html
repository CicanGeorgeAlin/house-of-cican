GLOBAL CICAN COUNT AUDIT

THE 399 QUESTION

≈399

An external surname-distribution database currently reports approximately 399 people bearing the surname Cican worldwide.

HOUSE OF CICAN does not treat this as a census. It is an external benchmark to investigate.

What the external source reports

The external source's current distribution table reports 212 in Romania, 148 in Turkey, 17 in the United States, 9 in Indonesia, 7 in India, and 1 each in Brazil, Cyprus, England, Peru and Slovenia. It separately reports the Çiçan variant.

Our audit

Documented people: maintained by HOUSE OF CICAN after identity review.

Occurrences: individual documentary mentions that may not yet represent distinct people.

Unresolved candidates: evidence exists but identity is not resolved.

External estimates: kept separate from our verified count.

Coverage gaps: countries, periods, archives and inaccessible records still requiring research.

Internal consistency check

The current external page says approximately 399 and says the surname occurs in 11 countries, but the visible country incidence values in the captured table sum to 398 and show Romania plus 9 other countries. HOUSE OF CICAN records this discrepancy as an audit item rather than silently correcting it.

The rule

We do not try to beat 399 by inflating our numbers. We try to determine what the evidence actually supports. A final number may be higher, lower, or impossible to establish precisely.
