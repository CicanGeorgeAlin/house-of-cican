CICAN NAME TIMELINE

A living evidence timeline for the earliest currently discovered Cican/Cicican name occurrences. An earlier surviving record can move the benchmark backward.

26 MAY 1547
CicanCicicanIurghiceniMoldavia

Earliest currently documented occurrence in this archive. The BNaR catalogue records Cican and son Illea in a princely confirmation concerning part of Iurghiceni. The scholarly transcription preserves the historical form Cicican.

What it proves: a documentary occurrence by 1547. What it does not prove: that this is the absolute first Cican in human history, the origin of the surname, or that the name was already hereditary in the modern sense.

Three different “firsts”

First discovered name occurrence — earliest surviving documentary spelling currently found.

First discovered identifiable person — earliest occurrence where the individual can be responsibly distinguished.

First evidence of hereditary surname use — earliest evidence demonstrating inherited family-name function.

These dates may differ. HOUSE OF CICAN will never collapse them into one unsupported “first Cican” claim.
