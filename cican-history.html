Cican · Cicican · documentary evidence

CICAN
SURNAME HISTORY

House of Cican currently treats the following as the earliest defensible documentary occurrence located in its research: a Moldavian charter dated 26 May 1547 concerning property in Iurghiceni.

26 May 1547 — IurghiceniThe BNaR historical archive catalogue identifies a charter in which Iliaș Voivode confirms rights involving Cican and his son Illea. The scholarly Documenta Romaniae Historica transcription renders the personal designation as Cicican. The underlying record is an original Slavonic document with a princely seal.

What this proves

It proves that a person recorded as Cican/Cicican and his son Illea appear in a documented Moldavian property record in 1547.

What it does not prove

It does not by itself prove that 1547 is the origin of the Cican surname, that every later Cican descends from this person, or that similarly spelled place names and people belong to the same family.

Why spelling matters

House of Cican preserves Cican and Cicican as documentary variants rather than silently normalizing them. Historical spelling, transcription and catalogue terminology can differ, so the archive records each form with its source.

Iurghiceni continuity research

Later Iurghiceni records from 1591–1592, 1611 and 1619 are being investigated as a documentary continuity chain. They are not currently merged with the 1547 Cican family without evidence connecting the people.

Current research question

The project is investigating whether the early designation was a hereditary surname, personal name, patronymic, nickname or another form of identification, and whether earlier records can be found.
