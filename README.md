# HOUSE OF CICAN

## Global Surname Archive

**Mission:** build a continuously expanding, evidence-first historical and genealogical archive of the Cican surname and everyone who can be responsibly documented as connected to it.

> **LEAVE NO CICAN BEHIND.**

House of Cican is not merely a personal family tree. It is intended to become a long-term public research archive covering:

- surname history and variants
- documented people and families
- places and historical geography
- migrations and diaspora
- births, baptisms, marriages and deaths
- military, property, occupational and civic records
- newspapers, books, manuscripts and archival collections
- photographs and documents where publication is permitted
- competing surname-origin hypotheses
- research leads, contradictions and rejected claims
- contributions from Cican families and researchers worldwide

### Research standard

Every important claim should be traceable to evidence. Uncertainty remains visible. Historical spellings are preserved. Conflicting evidence is recorded instead of silently resolved.
